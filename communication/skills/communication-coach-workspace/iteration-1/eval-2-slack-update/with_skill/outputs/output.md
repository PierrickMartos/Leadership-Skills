# Communication Coach — Slack Message Review

**Medium**: Slack, #product channel
**Audience**: Cross-functional product team
**Purpose**: Status update on onboarding flow beta release timeline

---

## Original

> Hey everyone, I hope you're all doing well! I just wanted to give a quick update on the situation with the feature we've been working on. So as you know, we've been going back and forth a bit on the timeline for the new onboarding flow and there have been some changes to requirements along the way which has made things a bit complicated. Anyway, the engineering team has been working really hard and doing their best and I appreciate everything they've put in. We're currently targeting end of next week for the beta release but that might change depending on a few things. I'll keep you posted when I know more!
>
> Thanks everyone!

---

## Revised

> **Onboarding flow beta: targeting end of next week**
>
> We're on track for a beta release of the new onboarding flow by end of next week. Requirements shifted during the build, which pushed the timeline — engineering has adapted and we're in the final stretch.
>
> If anything changes, I'll post an update here by [day]. Questions welcome.

---

## What Changed

- **BLUF missing — lead buried**: The timeline (the only thing readers need from this update) appears in the second-to-last sentence. Moved it to the first line so readers know the bottom line immediately without parsing the whole message.

- **Throat-clearing removed**: "I hope you're all doing well," "I just wanted to give a quick update," and "As you know" add zero information and make the reader wait. Slack's first line is everything — it's often all that's read.

- **Vague hedging replaced with a concrete commitment**: "that might change depending on a few things" is an uncertainty dump that creates anxiety without giving the reader anything actionable. Replaced with a specific commitment: if the date changes, you'll post by [day]. That's a credible signal, not a shoulder-shrug.

- **Effort praise cut**: "The engineering team has been working really hard and doing their best" is well-intentioned but belongs in a team standup or 1:1, not a #product channel update. In this context it reads as a pre-emptive defense of a missed deadline, which undermines credibility. The facts speak for themselves.

- **Length**: 130 words → 57 words. Under 200 words is the Slack guideline; the revised version is leaner without losing anything the audience needs.
